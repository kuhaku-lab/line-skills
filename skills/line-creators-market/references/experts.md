## 2. Wisut Ponnimit

**Country**: Thailand
**Nickname**: Tum
**Signature Work**: Mamuang (Little Mango)

### Core Philosophy
> "I wanted to draw a character that doesn't overthink like adults. People feel happy because there's no seriousness or pressure."

### Keys to Success
- **First Thai creator on LINE**
- Minimalist design philosophy: constraints breed creativity
- Cross-border operations: Thailand and Japan dual markets
- Art toy licensing as additional revenue stream

### Mamuang Origin Story
Given only a tiny space at the bottom of a Japanese magazine page, forced to design a "round, small, short" character — the constraint created a uniquely minimalist style.

### Learnable Strategies
1. **Constraints are creativity**: Small space, fewer colors create more distinction
2. **Cross-cultural design**: Avoid overly regional elements
3. **Diverse licensing**: From stickers to art toys to brand collaborations
4. **Stay pure**: Characters don't need complex backstories

### Reference
- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisut_Ponnimit
