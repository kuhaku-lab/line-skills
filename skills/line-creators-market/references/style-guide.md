# LINE Sticker Creative Style Guide

Five mainstream style directions, each with successful representative creators (see experts.md for details).

---

## 1. Healing / Kawaii

**Representatives**: sakumaru (Usamaru), Kanahei (Piske & Usagi), Machiko

### Visual Characteristics
- Rounded, simple outlines
- Soft color palette (pink, beige, light blue)
- Subtle but emotionally resonant expressions
- Occasional surreal humor

### Design Principles
- Character proportions lean chibi (large head, small body)
- Soft lines with no sharp angles
- Color palette limited to 3-4 main colors
- White space creates breathing room

### Best Markets
- Japan (core market)
- Taiwan, Korea (high demand for healing content)
- Global (universal emotions)

---

## 2. Minimalist

**Representatives**: Wisut Ponnimit (Mamuang), Moonlab (Maltese Dog)

### Visual Characteristics
- Extremely simplified lines
- Limited colors (2-3)
- White space sparks imagination
- Legible even at small sizes

### Design Principles
- Convey maximum emotion with minimum lines
- Remove all unnecessary detail
- Character identity relies on silhouette, not decoration
- Color serves as accent, not fill

### Best Markets
- Cross-border markets (not text-dependent)
- Younger demographics
- Design-conscious users

### Origin Story Insight
Mamuang was born because Wisut only had a tiny space at the bottom of a magazine page, forcing a "round, small, short" character. **Constraint breeds creativity.**

---

## 3. Story-Driven

**Representatives**: Nagano (Chiikawa)

### Visual Characteristics
- Characters have complete backstories
- Continuity across sticker sets
- Multi-character universe with interactions
- Expressions come from story context, not isolated poses

### Design Principles
- Develop the story first, then extend to stickers
- Characters have defined relationships
- Each new sticker set advances character development
- Social media serialization builds fan loyalty

### Best Markets
- Japan (deep storytelling culture)
- Creators with social media management skills
- Those targeting cross-media expansion (manga, anime)

### Business Model
```
Social media serialization → Build fanbase → Launch stickers → Feed back into story → Cross-media
```

---

## 4. Local Culture

**Representatives**: Machiko Papa (Lan Lan Cat)

### Visual Characteristics
- Incorporates local slang and trending phrases
- Reflects local cultural references
- Characters feel like a "local friend"
- Text is a core design element

### Design Principles
- Track latest trending phrases (PTT, Dcard, Twitter)
- Launch seasonal stickers ahead of competitors
- High-frequency updates maintain visibility
- Character family diversifies risk

### Best Markets
- Deep focus on single-language markets (Taiwan, Japan, Thailand)
- Requires ongoing cultural awareness
- Suited for high-output creators

### Notes
- Slang has a shelf life — requires continuous updates
- Slang cannot be directly translated across markets
- Requires deep understanding of target market culture

---

## 5. Request / Pleading

**Representatives**: Dogplease (BearPlease)

### Visual Characteristics
- Cute appearance paired with pleading poses
- Practical request / apology / gratitude expressions
- Exaggerated but endearing gestures
- Suited for workplace or family conversations

### Design Principles
- Function-first: users "need" to use it, not just "want" to
- Large gesture amplitude for direct emotional delivery
- Series development (bear please, cat please...)
- Same concept can be applied to different characters

### Best Markets
- Thailand / Southeast Asia (request culture)
- Workplace social scenarios
- Competition-oriented creators (high vote appeal)

### Business Model
```
Competition exposure → Social media accumulation → Series expansion → Continue competing
```

## Style Selection Decision Tree

```
What is your creative goal?
├── Cross-border sales → Minimalist / Healing (low text dependency)
├── Deep single-market focus → Local Culture (language is your weapon)
├── Build a long-term IP → Story-Driven (requires social media skills)
├── Quick launch & test → Request / Healing (proven formulas)
└── Artistic expression → Minimalist (constraint breeds creativity)
```

## Universal Design Principles

These principles apply regardless of style:

### Recognizability
- **Thumbnail test**: Character and expression must be identifiable at 96x74 px
- **Memorability**: One feature is enough to recognize the character (color, shape, accessory)
- **Consistency**: All stickers in a set maintain unified style

### Practicality
- Essential expressions: greeting, thanks, apology, OK, NG, happy, sad, angry, love, goodbye
- Add 2-3 character-specific signature expressions
- Consider "in what situation would a user think of this sticker"

### Technical Considerations
- Line weight remains clear when scaled down
- Sufficient color contrast (avoid same-tone layering)
- Clean character edges on transparent background
- First frame of animated stickers must work as standalone static image
